Support

Something wrong, or an idea? You're in the right place.

Lyfe is in Turkish; button names are given as they appear in the app.

The quickest way to us: inside the app

  • Tap the thin line on the right edge of the screen, or swipe in from the edge; the menu opens.
  • Ladybug: report a problem. The screen you were on is added for you.
  • Lightbulb: send an idea.
  • Both are also under Ayarlar → Lyfe hakkında (Settings → About Lyfe).

We read every message.

Common questions

Where are my records?

In your iCloud account. They appear on every device signed in with the same Apple Account, and only you can reach them.

What happens with iCloud off?

Your records stay on that phone. Turn iCloud on and they sync by themselves.

How do I learn what's where?

Open the short tour again any time from Ayarlar → Lyfe'ı tanı (Settings → Get to know Lyfe).

How do I open the diary when Face ID can't read my face?

Set a 6-digit Lyfe passcode (lyfe parolası) in Settings (Ayarlar). When your face can't be read, Lyfe asks for it.

My voice note wasn't transcribed.

Transcription happens on the phone, and the first time it may need to download the language file. Try again a little later; if it still fails, tell us with the ladybug.

Lyfe isn't on my Apple Watch.

In the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll down, find Lyfe and tap Install. Open Lyfe on the phone once so the watch can see your accounts.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleted records stay in Silinenler (Deleted) for 15 days; you can bring them back from there.
