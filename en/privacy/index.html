Privacy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Lyfe is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that keeps your notes, spending, places, moods and days in one place. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to those records.

Lyfe is in Turkish; button names are given as they appear in the app.

  • Your records live in your own iCloud account. Only you can reach them.
  • We receive only the two things you choose to send: Report a problem (Sorun bildir) and Send an idea (Öneri gönder).
  • Lyfe is ad-free.

Where your records live

Your notes, spending, accounts, shopping list, trips, moods and diary are stored in Apple's iCloud, in the private area of your account. Photos, videos and audio are written to the Lyfe folder in your iCloud Drive. With iCloud off, everything stays on your phone.

That area opens only to your Apple Account, so you are the only person who reads your records.

Which permission, and why

Each permission is asked the first time you use its feature, and it turns on only that feature. The rest of the app works on its own.

  • Location: to add where you wrote a note. If you turn on trips, Lyfe asks "you were here, save it?" after you stay somewhere for 20 minutes. Location stays on your phone and in your iCloud.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to record and transcribe sound in voice notes, videos and the video diary. The microphone listens only when you press record. Transcription happens on the phone.
  • Camera: to add a video to a note and to record the video diary. Videos are saved to your iCloud folder.
  • Photos: to suggest photos you took where you are writing the note. Suggestions are prepared on the phone; only the photo you tap is copied into the note.
  • Health: to show your steps, sleep and exercise on the day page, and today's steps and calories on the watch. These are shown on screen only. If you turn it on in mood settings, your moods are also written to Health as "State of Mind".
  • Calendar: to show the day's events on the day page. Events are only read.
  • Notifications: for shopping, salary and mood reminders. Reminders are set up on the phone.
  • Face ID: to open the video diary and the salary record. Face recognition happens on the iPhone itself; Lyfe receives only the result. You can add an optional 6-digit Lyfe passcode (lyfe parolası) as a backup. It is kept only in this phone's keychain, as a one-way hash.

What reaches us

We receive only the two things you choose to send.

Report a problem (the ladybug in the menu):

  • the note you write and the screen you were on;
  • the app version, iOS version and device model;
  • the app's log for the last 24 hours, which holds only outcomes and counts;
  • up to five crash or hang reports your phone collected, if any.

Send an idea (the lightbulb in the menu): only the text you write.

These arrive anonymously and are kept in Apple's iCloud, in an area only the people who make Lyfe can read. They are used only to fix and improve Lyfe.

Deleting

A record you delete in the app stays in Deleted (Silinenler) for 15 days, then it is removed for good. To remove all of Lyfe's iCloud data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Lyfe on your iPhone.

To have a report you sent us deleted, write to us with the ladybug in the app.

Children

Lyfe is designed for adults.

Changes

If this text changes, the date at the top is updated. An important change is also announced on the app's what's new (yenilikler) screen.
